Accessibility

What accessibility mode changes: higher contrast, a clear focus ring, larger tags and wider lines, no motion, and every gesture as a button.

How to turn it on: the switch on the start screen, or the accessibility button on the map. Both set the same mode.

The camera pad, bottom left over the map: the arrows turn and tilt, plus and minus zoom, and Pan makes the arrows move the map.

In view lists what is on the map now, each with a number that matches a badge on the map. Pick one to raise it or open it.

In a quiz, choose an answer, then Check. A find question takes the number of the thing on the map; Skip moves on without counting it.

Walkthroughs stop at the end of each step; Play goes on.
