Not for navigation

skycake is a study aid. Charts, airspace, procedures and terrain on skycake.app are not current, complete or accurate enough for flight planning or navigation. Plan and fly with current FAA charts and publications.

Data

Charts, airspace, procedures and diagrams come from the FAA, terrain and park outlines from the USGS, all public domain and shown as published, then simplified to draw in 3D. Procedures are drawn from coded legs with simple assumptions, not flown profiles. Data is rebuilt every 28 days and may be out of date, incomplete or wrong in between.
