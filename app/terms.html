Not for navigation

skycake is for study only. Never use skycake.app to plan or fly a flight, for situational awareness in the cockpit, or for terrain and obstacle clearance. The map shows no NOTAMs, temporary flight restrictions, weather or ATC changes, and a line on the map is not a safe path. The pilot in command is responsible for each flight and for using current official FAA charts and publications.

Data and offline copies

Charts, airspace, procedures, airport diagrams and procedure charts come from the FAA, terrain and park outlines from the USGS, all public domain. Charts are cropped, reprojected and joined at their edges, and procedure charts are shown in greyscale and turned upright. Airports and procedures near an area's edge may be left out, and heights can be stretched. Procedures are drawn from coded legs with simple climb and turn assumptions, not flown paths.

Updates are best effort and may stop. "Current through" names the FAA cycle an area was built from and does not make the display complete or valid for flight. A copy saved for offline use stays on the device until updated or removed, and can be older than the site.
